verse: "I. The Abyss\nI was born beneath the weight of water,\nwhere the world was nothing but silence and salt.\nThe deep\
  \ curled its fingers around my ribs,\ntightened like a clenched fist, unyielding.\n\nWhales whispered through the void,\n\
  their voices like distant thunder,\ntelling of a sun I had never touched,\na sky I could not fathom.\n\nBut the ocean does\
  \ not love its children.\nIt keeps them.\nIt sinks them.\nIt forgets them.\n\nII. The Fracture \nAbove, the ceiling of the\
  \ world trembled,\na trembling sheet of hammered glass.\nThen— a wound of light.\nA raw, golden gash torn through the dark,\n\
  spilling fire into the abyss.\n\nThe current shuddered.\nThe deep exhaled.\nSomething inside me broke.\n\nI kicked against\
  \ the drowning hush,\nagainst the weight of centuries,\nagainst the silence that had made a home in my lungs.\n\nThe water\
  \ clawed at my skin,\nsnarling, dragging, screaming—\nbut I rose.\nI rose.\n\nIII. The Breach \nThen— the shattering.\n\
  I tore through the sea like a blade through glass,\nripped from the deep in a cascade of silver and defiance,\nflung into\
  \ a sky so vast it made me tremble.\n\nWater crashed from my limbs like falling stars,\nreturning to the world that had\
  \ tried to keep me.\nBut I was no longer its prisoner.\n\nThe wind seized my breath and made it its own.\nThe sun carved\
  \ me in gold and fire.\n\nI was no longer of the sea.\nI was the ember of a drowned sun.\nI was the echo of forgotten storms.\n\
  I was the horizon unchained.\n\nAnd I would never fall again.\n\n27 February 2025"
